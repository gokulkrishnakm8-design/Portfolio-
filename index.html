FitDiet

A personalized food diet scheduling and fitness nutrition web application

FitDiet is my college project for gym users. A person enters their body weight and workout details, and the app returns a daily meal schedule: breakfast, lunch, pre-workout, post-workout and dinner.

I'm building it with VS Code, Git and GitHub, and giving the site a dark, cinematic look.

  • Web app
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Git and GitHub
  • Meal scheduling

    A small preview of the idea: meals arranged around your workout time. It is not nutrition advice.

    Fitness and nutrition

    Training

    I follow gym training, which is why FitDiet starts from workout information.

    Food timing

    What you eat before and after a session matters, so the planner treats those meals as their own slots.

    Consistency

    A schedule you can follow each day beats a perfect plan you drop. I want the app to make that easy.
